This app (“the App”) is a private desktop tool operated by {{OWNER}}. It is used only by its owner to prepare marketing content (videos and photo carousels) and send it as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. The App is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

When the TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives only what the granted scopes allow:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send videos and photos to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published automatically; the account owner reviews and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

The App does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics, contacts or any other TikTok data.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to connect the owner's account and send the owner's own content to their TikTok inbox at the scheduled time. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

  • Tokens and account details are stored locally on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are never sent to a third-party server.
  • Photos for carousels are temporarily hosted at {{PREFIX}} so that TikTok can retrieve them, as required by the TikTok Content Posting API. They are the owner's own marketing images and contain no personal data.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, through its official APIs, to perform the actions described above.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account remains connected. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or by disconnecting the account in the App, which deletes the stored tokens.

6. Children

The App is not intended for, and does not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above reflects the latest version. For any question, contact the operator through {{SITE}}.
